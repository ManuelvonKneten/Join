Overview

Join is a student project developed as part of a web development bootcamp at the Developer Akademie GmbH. This privacy policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it in accordance with the DSGVO, the Bundesdatenschutzgesetz (BDSG), and the Telekommunikation-Telemedien-Datenschutz-Gesetz (TTDSG). Join is intended for educational purposes only and is not a commercial product.

Data We Collect

When you register for Join, we collect the following personal data (§ 3 Abs. 1 BDSG, Art. 4 Nr. 1 DSGVO):

  • Name
  • E-mail address
  • Password (stored in encrypted form via Firebase Authentication)

When you use Join, we additionally store task and contact data that you enter yourself, such as task titles, descriptions, due dates, and contact names.

Access to and storage of information on your end device requires your consent pursuant to § 25 Abs. 1 TTDSG, unless it is strictly necessary for the provision of the service (§ 25 Abs. 2 TTDSG).

Purpose and Legal Basis

We process your personal data solely to provide the functionality of Join, specifically to authenticate your account and to save and display your tasks and contacts. The legal basis for this processing is your consent pursuant to Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO, given when you create an account.

You may withdraw your consent at any time pursuant to Art. 7 Abs. 3 DSGVO by contacting us at the address listed above. The withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out on the basis of the consent prior to its withdrawal.

Firebase / Google

Join uses Firebase, a platform provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Firebase is used for user authentication (Firebase Authentication) and data storage (Firestore). Google acts as a data processor within the meaning of Art. 28 DSGVO; a data processing agreement (Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag) has been concluded with Google.

Your data may be transferred to Google servers in third countries. Such transfers are based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses pursuant to Art. 46 Abs. 2 lit. c DSGVO. For more information, see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy .

Data Retention

Your personal data will be deleted or restricted as soon as the purpose of storage no longer applies, pursuant to Art. 17 DSGVO and § 35 BDSG. Your account data is retained for as long as your account exists. Task and contact data is retained until you delete it or your account is removed. Because Join is a student project, all data may be deleted without notice when the project is discontinued.

Your Rights

Under the DSGVO and the BDSG, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 DSGVO, § 34 BDSG) — you may request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 DSGVO) — you may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 DSGVO, § 35 BDSG) — you may request deletion of your data ("Recht auf Vergessenwerden").
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 DSGVO) — you may request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 DSGVO) — you may request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object (Art. 21 DSGVO) — you may object to the processing of your data at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the address listed above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority (Datenschutzaufsichtsbehörde) pursuant to Art. 77 DSGVO in conjunction with § 19 BDSG if you believe that the processing of your personal data violates the DSGVO.

No Automated Decision-Making

Join does not use automated decision-making or profiling as defined under Art. 22 DSGVO.
